ShowA comme Animal

Visual theatre · Available in French and English

Synopsis

An old philosopher is about to share his thoughts, but suddenly stops when he bites into an apple and falls asleep. Like Alice in Wonderland, he is swept into a dream where anything is possible!

Guided and deftly manipulated by three performers transformed into fantastic animal figures, the man comes apart and comes back together as he explores his childhood memories. Woven in the spirit of fables and fairy tales, this visual theatre piece, freely inspired by Gilles Deleuze’s lectures, is an ode to imagination, impermanence and the search for identity. It invites audiences of all ages to be carried away by a unique world that is funny, poetic and delightfully unsettling.

Artistic direction

Marie-Pascale
Bélanger

A multidisciplinary artist, Marie-Pascale Bélanger holds degrees in visual arts and in dance performance from the École de danse contemporaine de Montréal (EDCM). She holds a master’s degree in dance from UQAM, focused on integrating images into the stage space, as well as a graduate diploma in contemporary puppet theatre (UQAM, 2019). Her career has led her to create choreographic works presented in Canada, the United States and Europe.

Artistic approach

TrainingEDCM, visual arts and dance Master’sDance, UQAM Graduate diplomaContemporary puppet theatre, UQAM, 2019

Her practice is rooted in interdisciplinary research where dance, visual arts and puppetry meet. Drawing on a visual aesthetic shaped by comics and surrealism, she explores stage devices that blur the line between the real and the fantastic. Driven by a hunger for material and transformation, she designs and builds her own sets and puppets, which she sees as dramaturgical agents in their own right. Each stage object thus takes part in a visual and physical writing that puts body, object and image in tension and shifts perception, inviting the audience into a sensory experience where familiar landmarks move and reassemble.

CollaboratorsThe team

Puppeteer

Marika
Karlsson

An artist and puppeteer, Marika Karlsson began her theatre journey in Finland at a very young age. After studying music and drama, she went on to earn a BFA in acting at Concordia University in Tiohtià:ke/Montreal. She completed UQAM’s graduate diploma in contemporary puppet theatre in 2021. Her work draws on somatic and multidisciplinary approaches, and her artistic research explores the temporality of the human body.

Performer · A comme Animal

A 1998 graduate of the Ateliers de danse moderne de Montréal, Elinor Fueter has taken part in many dance projects as well as street theatre, film and improvisation. She has performed in works produced by Danse-Cité and by numerous choreographers, including Lina Cruz, Jean-Pierre Perreault, Catherine Lipscombe, Nathalie Morin, Chantal Wadge, Alain Francœur, Peter James and the Mobile Home company. For about ten years she has been part of the Montréal Danse team. Recently, she has appeared in works by Benoît Lachambre, George Stamos, Caroline Laurin-Beaucage and Karine Ledoyen.

Music composition

Rodrigo
Rojas

Originally from Chile, Rodrigo trained in classical piano before quickly moving into electroacoustic composition. Deeply moved by the northern landscapes of James Bay when he arrived in Canada, he still carries the impression that wild nature left on him. By playing with and distorting sound, he creates musical worlds that unfold like soundscapes. Alongside his work with the duo Mentasta, his scores have been heard on theatre stages as well as in contemporary dance.
