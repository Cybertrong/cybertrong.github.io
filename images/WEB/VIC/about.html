about

I am a photographer graduated from the Marsan school in Montreal. Born in Canada with Spanish and Vietnamese origins, these rich and different cultural influences allowed me to open up to the wide horizons of creativity. I have a particular attraction for studio photography and the mastery of light. Working with stylists or artistic directors with a modern and pioneering approach to fashion is a stimulant that motivates me strongly. I also work with film cameras, which I develop and digitize myself.
