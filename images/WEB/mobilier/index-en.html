Commercial Design

Commercial furniture designed to last, evolve and adapt

This project proposes an approach to commercial furniture where flexibility, durability, and circularity are integrated from the design stage.

Designed for shopping centers and high-traffic environments, it enables support for diverse uses — from marketing activations to pop-up shops — while reducing single-use furniture logic.

The result: a modular, robust, and visually cohesive solution, aligned with new expectations for commercial spaces.

Approach

Why this approach

Today's commercial spaces must stand out, transform, and reinvent themselves continuously. They become destinations where commerce, culture, events, and experiences intersect. This evolution demands tools capable of keeping pace without compromising quality or site identity.

Furniture plays a key role in this transformation. When well-designed, it structures uses, supports programming, and strengthens a space's signature.

This proposal follows this logic: create furniture capable of evolving with the space, supporting multiple uses, and enabling a contemporary vision of commerce — flexible, coherent, and responsible.

Foundations

The pillars of our project

Designed for commercial realities

Furniture conceived from real commercial environment needs. Whether marketing activations, temporary kiosks, events, or common space usage, each module is developed to respond to real uses in demanding operational contexts. The goal: provide a reliable, functional, and easy-to-deploy solution suited to field teams and large-scale strategies alike.

Flexible, modular, and evolutionary

One furniture family, multiple configurations. Modules are designed to transform and adapt according to needs: enclosed kiosk, demonstration space, workshop table, reception counter, or sales island. This flexibility enables uses to evolve over time without multiplying purchases or compromising visual coherence.

A strong aesthetic signature

Furniture that actively contributes to the space's experience and image. Beyond functionality, each element offers consistent, contemporary, and distinctive visual presence. Together, they structure spaces, support activations, and strengthen site identity while maintaining high perceived quality.

Circularity integrated from design

An approach where durability and performance work hand in hand. The project integrates circularity principles from the start: material choices, robustness, repairability, and reuse potential. The goal is not just reducing impact, but proposing a solution sustainable over time, aligned with ESG commitments, without compromising aesthetics or uses.

A solution designed to combine coherence and adaptation.

The furniture structure remains constant to ensure deployment efficiency and overall clarity.

Customization options — finishes, materials, visual identity, and interior organization — allow each unit to be adapted to specific user needs.

The integration of materials from resource centers enriches this flexibility by offering recycled or low-impact alternatives, aligned with a tangible circular approach.

This approach transcends simple furniture. It proposes a way to support current commercial uses while affirming a more sustainable, flexible, and inspiring vision of spaces.
